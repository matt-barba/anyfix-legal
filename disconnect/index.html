Disconnect AnyFix Books

Access can be withdrawn at any time, and takes effect immediately.

From QuickBooks Online

Sign in to QuickBooks Online, then go to Settings and open Apps. Find AnyFix Books in the list of connected apps and choose Disconnect. Once disconnected, the integration can no longer read or write anything in the company file.

This is the authoritative way to end access, because it is performed from the QuickBooks account itself and does not depend on the integration cooperating.

From the integration

Because AnyFix Books runs locally rather than as a hosted service, the stored credentials also live locally. Revoking the token at Intuit's OAuth revocation endpoint ends the session, and removing the entries from the macOS Keychain under the service name anyfix-qbo removes the stored client ID, client secret, refresh token and company id from the machine.

What happens to data

The integration stores no copy of the accounting data. Records live in QuickBooks Online and are unaffected by disconnecting. The only local artifacts are the credentials described above and an error log containing API fault messages, which holds no credentials and no accounting records.
